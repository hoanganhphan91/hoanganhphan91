Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Hoang Anh Phan / Exsoft Mobile Apps (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile applications (collectively, the “Applications”), including utility tools such as Flashlight, Weather, Calculator, Compass, Camera/Scanner tools, Games, and related utilities published under Google Play developer account Exsoft.

1. Information We Collect

A. Location Information

Certain Applications (such as Weather and Compass apps) may request access to your device’s Precise (GPS) or Approximate location solely to deliver core services like local weather forecasts, cardinal directions, and altitude data. Location data is processed in real time and is never sold or used for tracking across third-party apps.

B. Camera & Storage Access

Certain Applications (such as PDF Scanner, QR Scanner, Badminton Camera) require access to your device's Camera or local storage strictly to scan documents, capture match videos, or decode QR codes. All processing and photos remain local on your device unless you explicitly choose to share or export them.

C. Device & Diagnostic Information

We automatically receive technical diagnostic information (device model, Android OS version, app version, language, crash logs) to maintain application stability, fix bugs, and optimize performance.

D. Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

Our Applications display advertisements powered by the Google AdMob SDK. AdMob may collect and process information such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, and ad interaction metrics in strict compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2. How We Use Information

  • To deliver and maintain core application features.
  • To detect, prevent, and fix technical crashes and errors.
  • To serve non-intrusive advertisements via Google AdMob in accordance with Google policies.
  • We do not collect personal identification information (PII) such as your name, phone number, or home address.

3. Third-Party Services

Our Applications integrate trusted third-party services:

  • Google Play Services: Core Android platform infrastructure.
  • Google AdMob: Mobile advertising provider (Google Privacy Policy).

4. Children’s Privacy

Our Applications are intended for general audiences (ages 13 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Security & Data Retention

We do not store your personal data on external servers. Diagnostic data and ad metrics processed by Google services are protected by Google's industry-standard encryption protocols.
