  • Retro Island Hero: 8-Bit Run (com.exsoft.retroislandrun)

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information that may be collected while you enjoy our games.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information

We do not collect, store, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), such as your legal name, physical address, phone number, contacts, or financial details. Our games are designed to be played immediately without requiring account creation, registration, or login credentials.

B. Device & Technical Diagnostic Information

To ensure game performance, smooth frame rates, and crash resolution across different Android hardware, we and our service providers may automatically collect anonymous technical information. This includes:

  • Device manufacturer, model, and screen resolution
  • Android operating system version
  • Application version and installation metrics
  • Anonymous crash logs and performance telemetry

C. Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

Our games may display in-game advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may process non-sensitive device identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID), IP address (solely for general geographic location to serve appropriate regional ads), and ad interaction statistics in strict compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2. How We Use Information

  • To deliver enjoyable, stable, and responsive retro arcade gameplay.
  • To detect, troubleshoot, and fix game bugs, crashes, or rendering issues.
  • To serve non-intrusive advertisements in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.
  • We never sell, trade, or monetize your data with third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Our Applications utilize trusted Google services:

  • Google Play Services: Essential Android gaming infrastructure (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google AdMob: Monetization and advertising provider (Google Ads Privacy).

4. Children’s Privacy

Our games are built for general audiences of all ages. We do not intentionally collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal information has inadvertently been provided, please contact us immediately and we will take prompt steps to remove it.

5. Security & Data Retention

All in-game save data (such as high scores, unlocked levels, and retro audio settings) is stored locally on your device storage. We do not maintain external servers to collect your personal records.
