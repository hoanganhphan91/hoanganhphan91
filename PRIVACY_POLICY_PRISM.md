## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
We **do not collect, store, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII)** such as your name, address, phone number, contacts, or financial details. Our games can be played without creating an account or logging in.

### B. Device & Technical Diagnostic Information
To ensure game stability and optimize performance across Android devices, anonymous technical data may be processed:
- Device manufacturer, model, and screen resolution
- Android OS version
- App version and installation metrics
- Anonymous crash reports and performance telemetry

### C. Advertising Data (Google AdMob)
Our games display advertisements via the Google AdMob SDK. AdMob may process non-sensitive identifiers like the Google Advertising ID (GAID) and IP address in accordance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.

## 2. How We Use Information
- To deliver smooth retro arcade gameplay.
- To detect and fix crashes and bugs.
- To serve advertisements in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.
- We do not sell or monetize personal data.
